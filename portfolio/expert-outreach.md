# External Technical Feedback Plan

## Why this feedback is needed

Most of my current technical checking comes from my own audits, tests, live runs and published documentation. Those sources have improved the project, but they can share my blind spots. I need a relevant person to challenge a narrow, answerable question rather than ask for vague praise.

## Target person and question

**Potential reviewers:** a local IT/security professional, a university/TAFE technology contact, or an experienced developer recommended through a community organisation.

**Question:** “I am a Year 11 student completing SACE Stage 2 AIF. I am building Kobald, a research assistant where state-changing actions require human approval and actions are recorded. From this short diagram or demo, what is one failure mode or safety control I should test next?”

## Draft message

> Hello [Name],
>
> I am a Year 11 student working on a SACE Stage 2 AIF project called Kobald. I am learning how to build an AI research assistant that can investigate tasks but requires human approval before anything state-changing happens.
>
> I am not asking you to endorse the project or share confidential information. If you have 10–15 minutes, could you look at a short, redacted explanation and answer one question: what safety failure mode or control should I test next?
>
> I will record your feedback, decide what I can realistically apply, and explain the result in my school portfolio. Thank you for considering it.

## Follow-up and response record
